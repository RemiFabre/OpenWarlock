Lobby UI art (portraits, difficulty icons, gems, decor, READY plaques), the 20
illustrated player avatars in avatars/, and the 37 shop artworks in shop/ (one
per spell, element and item).

Contributed by @SamKhosravi for issue #14 (the v4 lobby pack, the v5 avatar pack
and the v7 shop pack), generated at his request with OpenAI image generation. Confirmed on the issue
(2026-08-14, all three packs) as his to license: no third-party or licensed
game material. MIT, like the rest of the project.

Downscaled for runtime: the two READY plaques, the crest, the small divider and
the badge to twice their display size, the avatars to 128 px (also twice their
largest use), the shop artwork to 144 px (three times its 48 px card slot).
Roughly 70 MB of source art ships as ~2.8 MB.
