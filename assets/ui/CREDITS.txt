Lobby UI art (portraits, difficulty icons, gems, decor, READY plaques), the 23
illustrated player avatars in avatars/, the 37 shop artworks in shop/ (one per
spell, element and item), and the arena set in arena/ (floor, victory, defeat,
gold).

Contributed by @SamKhosravi for issue #14 (the v4 lobby pack, the v5 and v8 avatar
packs, the v7 shop pack and the v8 arena pack), generated at his request with OpenAI image generation. Confirmed on the issue
(2026-08-14, all three packs) as his to license: no third-party or licensed
game material. MIT, like the rest of the project.

Downscaled for runtime: the two READY plaques, the crest, the small divider and
the badge to twice their display size, the avatars to 128 px (also twice their
largest use), the shop artwork to 144 px (three times its 48 px card slot).
Roughly 70 MB of source art ships as ~2.8 MB.

The arena character sheets in knight/ are the "2D HD Character Knight" pack
supplied by @SamKhosravi for issue #14 (v9 animation prototype): Idle, Walk,
Run, CastSpell, TakeDamage and Die, 1920x1024 sheets of 128px cells, 15 frames
by 8 directions. Shipped as delivered, no re-encode.
