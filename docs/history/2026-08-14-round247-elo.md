# Round 24.7 elo: first measurement of the 53-row roster (2026-08-14)

The first standard run on the reworked roster (report + design:
`2026-08-14-round247-roster-rework.md`). Remi's 24.7 ruling applied: the
per-build sample is the constant, not the total, so 2500 games (was 2000 at
42 rows) keeps ~190 seats/row. One seed, seed 1, per the standing ruling.
Interactive page (same data, hoverable builds):
`docs/history/2026-08-14-elo-2500g-seed1.html`.

## How to read this

Elo fitted by Bradley-Terry over every pairwise placement in 2500 random
4-of-roster berserker (Hard) lobbies, seed 1, elemental. 1500 = roster
average, +173 ≈ a 73% pairwise favourite, neighbours within ~40 are noise
(seats/row ≈ 190). games = seats played, place = mean finishing place of 4.
Bot read ONLY: bots do not lead targets, bait, or chain CC windows;
contagion and positioning value read at a floor; anger saturates bot
instruments. Family K rows ride the FAKER brain (that gap is the point).

## The headline reads (all vs the same-shell control, stated per line)

- **The driving gap is intact and huge**: the five Faker rows hold ranks
  1-5 (2438-2919), ~+960 to +1440 over D1-warlord (1479), the Hard-brain
  base build. K5 (anger on Faker) repeats as overall #1.
- **Family G (one variable vs D1-warlord 1479):**
  - **+Blood Debt = +243** (G2 1722, rank 6, best non-Faker row).
  - **+Shield = +159** (G1 1638). Debt beats Shield by ~84 at identical
    cost/slots. ⚠ BOT ARTIFACT WARNING on both: since 24.6 Hard+ casts
    these on a clean imminent-ball read, i.e. near-perfect reflexes; a
    human will extract less. Flagged, not a buff/nerf signal on its own.
  - **no arcane = -23** (G4 1456): inside noise. Arcane 2 in the Warlord
    shell pays roughly its 12 g, no more.
  - **no sword = -94** (G3 1385): lifesteal-by-structure is real
    (question L), worth ~94 elo to the base build.
- **Family M (mutation maxed first, identical 166 g scaffold; read M vs M):**
  anger 1650 > malady 1611 > midas 1581 > vampire 1479 > echo 1417 >
  frost 1353. ~300 elo spread on an identical shell. Anger's lead is
  bot-saturated as always; frost last is consistent with bots not
  exploiting the freeze they create (no CC-chaining).
- **D2 vs D3 (the two mark hunts, identical chase shell):** anger's
  permanent +0.5 dmg (D2 1407) out-earns midas's +2 g flat (D3 1205) by
  ~200. The gold claim underpays even with the 24.2 faster cadence; midas
  reads better on the M scaffold (M5 1581) where the extra gold feeds a
  real element curve, than in a chase shell built around claiming it.
- **D4-leech (frequency respec) 1520**: above D1 (1479), mid-pack. Not
  comparable to the pre-24.5 1603 (the dive costs those rows ~100, by
  design) nor to the old damage-core row; this is the new baseline.
- **D13-bastion 1355 vs D8-juggernaut 1186**: the two reactive buttons are
  worth ~170 over armor-only, but a full defensive identity still sits
  ~120 under the roster average. Consistent with the offense-first meta
  (question H).
- **Floor check**: A2-items-mobility (1073) and D6-sumo (1103) hold the
  bottom, unchanged in spirit from r368; no new row fell out of the world,
  no game failed to finish (0 unfinished).

floored; human timing is worse than a scripted read. Contagion spread,
gust positioning, mine/decoy play: still invisible. Faker rows measure the
combo layer plus superior driving, not builds a Hard bot could copy.
