# Strategy ELO — round 21.8 (2026-08-11, r232)

**What this measures.** 33 shopping strategies (the roster in `tools/roster.js`,
one buy list each, all padded into a 150-185 g band) meet in random 4-of-33
lobbies. Every seat is a **Hard (berserker) bot** — the strategies differ ONLY in
what they buy. A Bradley-Terry fit over every pairwise placement turns 8000 games
into one number per strategy, scaled so **1500 = the roster average** and
**+173 ≈ a 73% favourite in a head-to-head pair**. `place` is the mean finish
(1-4, lower is better) and is the raw, un-fitted view of the same thing.

⚠ **It is a RANKING, not a strength meter.** The fit pins the average at 1500, so
a row can gain 100 points purely because its rivals got worse — and this round
the roster itself grew from 30 to 33 entries, which moves everyone. Read the Δ
column as "where it sits among its peers now", never as "this got stronger".

**Instrument.** `node tools/elo.js --games=8000 --seed=1` and `--seed=7`, ~20 min
each, elemental, 0 unfinished games in both. **Cross-seed drift: mean 8.8 Elo,
max 30** — the tightest agreement any of these tables has shown, so a gap of
~50 points is real and a gap of ~10 is noise.

⚠ The previous table (`2026-08-10-round21-elo.md`) used seeds 1 and **2**; this
one uses 1 and **7**. The Δ column compares seed 1 to seed 1.

## Findings

Each of these is the table above plus one claim; the numbers quoted are seed-1
Elo unless stated, and every Δ carries the zero-sum caveat from the header.

1. **The Hat of Aura is this round's real winner — 4th of 33 on both seeds
   (1760/1766).** D12-hatburner is the new core: Hat of Aura maxed + malady
   maxed, both of which were buffed this round (the burn now lingers 3-5 s, and
   malady's levels buy 1 → 1.5 → 2 damage per tick). It is the only new content
   that measures strong, and it beat *every* pure damage or CDR build. The
   companion reading is D5-plaguebearer at **+129** (21st → 15th) — same plague,
   no Hat. So roughly: the malady rework is worth ~+130 and adding the Hat on top
   is worth ~+260 more.
   ⚠ Both are **floors**: bots never bunch up, so contagion spread barely
   happens, and neither build's aura is being *positioned*.

2. **Anger has not moved, again.** B3-mutation-depth holds #1 at 2087/2073
   (+23) and D2-executioner #3 at 1840 (−40) — three rounds of price passes and
   two new items have changed nothing about it. This table cannot tell you
   whether anger is *too strong*, only that it is still the best thing to buy;
   that judgement stays Remi's (BALANCE question K).

3. **Items finally moved as a class, and it took CONTENT, not price cuts.** Every
   item core gained: A2-items-mobility **+126**, B6-item-breadth **+118**,
   D8-juggernaut **+93**, B5-item-depth **+91**, A1-items-sustain **+58**. Three
   consecutive rounds of price cuts had moved them 0; adding two items that
   *do something* (a burn aura and a flat heal) moved them 60-130 points in one
   round. ⚠ They are still the bottom third — 21st, 22nd, 26th, 28th, 29th of 33
   — so the shelf is better, not fixed. The old finding stands, sharpened:
   **items are effect-limited, not price-limited.**

4. **The Slow Spoon's own build is 32nd of 33 (1187/1178) — but do not read that
   as "the spoon is weak".** D11-spoonbearer is deliberately the worst-case host
   for it: frost 3 + gale 3 is a *low-damage CC kit*, and a bot cannot convert
   CC into damage (it does not chain, bait, or follow up a freeze). C6-bolt-combo,
   the other frost/gale build, sits right next to it at 31st and always has. The
   same spoon inside item cores rose (+58 A1, +118 B6). Honest summary: this
   table priced the **kit**, not the item. The spoon needs a human game.

5. **Statue measures at exactly the roster average (1518/1500) — which is the
   right answer.** Bots own one reading of it: "I am under half HP and a ball is
   about to land, become gold for 2 s". That is worth precisely nothing and
   precisely not-negative, and the fit agrees to within noise of 1500. Its real
   value is choosing *when* to be untouchable, which no bot does.

6. **The meteor buff is invisible here.** C5-meteor-value is still dead last
   (1155, Δ −1) after lv2 damage went 24 → 30. Either +6 damage on one level of
   a 12 g spell is too small to matter, or — more likely — the CC-gated cast
   means bots rarely land it at all. If the meteor is supposed to be a real
   choice, the next lever is its cast rules, not its damage.

7. **Breadth builds all slid, and they slid together**: B2-element-breadth −100,
   A3-elements-only −101, D10-skirmisher −96, A5-spells-only −95, C3-kit-width
   −89, E2-chronomancer −70. With 33 rows pinned at an average of 1500, three
   new entries and a rising item shelf, this is mostly the pin moving under them
   — the ORDER among them is nearly unchanged. Do not retune off this line.

## What this instrument cannot see

- **The Mine and the Decoy are absent by construction.** A bot cannot set a trap
  it must bait someone onto, and cannot be fooled by a copy that is not a player.
  They are not in the roster and no number here prices them.
- **Contagion, gusts and positioning** — malady's spread, gale's shove into the
  lava, the Hat's ring placement — are all under-measured: bots do not cluster,
  do not aim shoves, and do not kite an aura.
- **Teams, permanent pillars, the arena scaling and the whole 21.3-21.6 feature
  set** never appear: every game here is a 4-seat free-for-all.
- **Human-only spells** (Switcheroo combos, vanish timing, Repulse baits, Mirror
  Wall) are excluded from the roster on purpose — bots own none of them.
