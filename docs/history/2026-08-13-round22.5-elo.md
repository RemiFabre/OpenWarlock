# Strategy ELO, round 22.4 + 22.5 (2026-08-13, r353)

**What this measures.** 41 shopping strategies (`tools/roster.js`) in random
4-of-41 lobbies; a Bradley-Terry fit over every pairwise placement, scaled so
**1500 = the roster average** and **+173 is a 73% pairwise favourite**. `place`
is the mean finish (1-4, lower is better). Seats are **Hard** bots except the
four K rows, which pin their own **Faker** brain. `node tools/elo.js
--games=8000 --seed=1` and `--seed=2`, elemental, **0 unfinished games** in
both. **Cross-seed drift mean 11.1, max 38**, so a gap of ~50 is real and ~15
is noise. Every mover quoted below was checked for **direction agreement on
both seeds**.

## ⚠ Six things changed between the r249 table and this one

Game changes (`docs/history/2026-08-11-round21.9-elo.md` is the r249 baseline):

1. **22.4 stack fade**: frost/gale/malady piles lose 1 stack per 9 unfed
   seconds (`STACK_DECAY`); reapply resets the clock, midas/anger exempt.
2. **22.4 reflected balls** keep the element's owner (`pr.elemOwner`).
3. **22.5 fireball range 50**, was Infinity.
4. **22.5 anger** `markEvery` [20,15,10] to **[30,25,20]**.
5. **22.5 vampire** engorged ball heals a **FLAT [10,20,30]** instead of
   [1.4, 1.92, 2.45] times the damage dealt.
6. **22.5 midas** `dmgMult` [0.5,0.75,1] to **[0.7,0.85,1]**.

And one INSTRUMENT change: the roster grew **37 to 41**. Family K (the Faker's
four combo arsenals, issue #7) joined, and those rows run the Faker brain in a
lobby of Hard bots.

## How to read the Δ column (it is not a raw subtraction)

Two corrections, both mandatory, or the table lies:

- **Re-centring.** Elo here is a fit over one roster, and the fit pins the
  average at 1500. The four Faker rows land at 2444-2755, so the mean of the
  37 rows that r249 also held fell to **1376**. Every raw Δ would read about
  −124 for that reason alone. The Δ printed below adds that 123.8 back, which
  makes both tables have mean 1500 **over the same 37 strategies**. (BT
  strengths are a ratio scale, so adding players shifts the normalisation, not
  the relative strengths.)
- **Zero-sum redistribution.** After re-centring the Δ column still sums to
  zero. The four anger rows lose 666 Elo between them, and that has to land
  somewhere: the **median move of the 32 non-anger, non-vampire rows is +19**.
  So read +19 as the "nothing happened to me" line, not zero. A row is an
  independent mover only when it is well clear of it.

## What moved, and whether it makes sense

### 1. The anger nerf is the whole table. It landed, hard, and monotonically.

Four rows hold anger, and they fell in exact proportion to how much of it they
hold. Both seeds agree on every one.

| strategy | what it buys | Δ |
|---|---|---|
| **B3-mutation-depth** | anger to lv3 FIRST, then Health Amulet 3 + Blood Sword 3, boots 2, cape 1 | **−267** |
| **D2-executioner** | anger 3 + ghost 3 (the chase kit) + boots 3, then sword 3 + amulet 3 | **−205** |
| **B4-mutation-breadth** | one level of anger/frost/midas/malady/Echo, then anger to 3 | **−104** |
| **A4-mutations-only** | anger 3, frost 3, malady 3, midas 3, Echo 3, zero items | **−90** |

B3 was **1st of 37 in r249 at 2004**, the row that had led every table since
round 20. It is now 6th of 41, and 2nd among the rows r249 also held. D2 fell
out of the top four to 17th. Slowing the mark from every 20/15/10 s to every
30/25/20 s cuts the claim rate by a third at lv1 and **a half at lv3**, and
since the ramp is permanent, half as many claims is half the accumulated
fireball damage for the whole back half of the game.

**Confirmed on a second instrument, and it does NOT close question K.** The
elemental study (800 games, 4 Hard warlord seats, one element each, win% vs a
25% baseline) run with the pre-22.5 mark timer against the shipped one:

| seed | anger, markEvery [20,15,10] | anger, shipped [30,25,20] |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 92.3% | **83.3%** |
| 7 | 91.0% | **84.1%** |

A real, both-seed, 7-9 point drop, and anger is **still the best element in the
game by 20 points** (vampire is 2nd at ~62%). This is the classic bot-inflation
shape from question K: bots claim marks near-perfectly, so the mirror cannot
price the human cost of hunting one specific target, which is the element's
whole design. **Flag it, do not number-nerf around it.** Remi's feel decides.

### 2. Vampire's flat heal is the biggest gain in the table, which is backwards from the intent but correct.

**D4-leech** (vampire 3 + Echo 3, then Blood Sword 3 + Health Amulet 3)
**+219**, the only large gain in the table, agreeing on both seeds
(+220 / +219). It is now 13th of 41, up from 27th of 37.

The change was a nerf in intent: the engorged ball healed 140/192/245% of the
damage it dealt, which scaled with the damage builds that already dominate.
Flat [10,20,30] removes that scaling. But D4 is deliberately a LOW-damage
vampire kit, and the fireball is locked at lv1 in elemental, so with no damage
element its engorged ball healed about **2.45 × 7 = 17 hp** and now heals a flat
**30**. The builds the change was aimed at are the ones that stack ember and
anger, and D4 is the ONLY vampire core in the roster, so the nerf hit nobody
here and the buff hit the one row that had it. That is the instrument being
honest about a roster gap, not a reason to touch the number.
⚠ Vampire could NOT be ablated: the old percentage path was deleted from
`sim.js` in 22.5, so restoring it needs a code revert, not an `--fx`.

### 3. Malady lost about what the fade should cost it. Frost and gale did not move.

The two plague builds are the only clearly-hurt non-anger rows:
**D12-hatburner** (Hat of Aura 3 + malady 3 + treads 2 + amulet 2) **−64**,
**D5-plaguebearer** (malady 3 + terra 3 + treads 2 + amulet 3) **−37**. Both
agree on both seeds. The frost and gale rows did not fall at all: two sit on the
+19 redistribution line (D11-spoonbearer, Slow Spoon 3 + frost 3 + gale 3, +12;
D6-sumo, gale 3 + cape 3, +12) and two are above it (C6-bolt-combo, frost 3 +
bolt 3 + gale 3 + Echo 3, +44; C5-meteor-value, frost 3 + meteor 2, +78, still
last but now 6 Elo behind C6 instead of r249's 38).

**The fade ablation says the same thing.** Elemental study, 800 games, win% vs
a 25% baseline, shipped 9 s fade against the fade switched off, same seeds and
lineups:

| element | s1 fade ON | s1 fade OFF | s7 fade ON | s7 fade OFF |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| malady | 54.6% | 59.5% | 51.0% | 58.3% |
| gale | 7.7% | 9.9% | 7.3% | 8.4% |
| frost | 13.4% | 13.8% | 7.0% | 11.7% |

Malady loses 4.9 and 7.3 points, direction agreeing. Frost and gale move less
than the instrument's own churn (vampire, which the fade cannot touch, moved
10.2 points between two runs on seed 7, so treat anything under ~7 points here
as unresolved).

**Why so little shows up at all.** A pile only fades when it goes 9 s unfed, and
a Hard berserker prowls a ring of 8.5 units around one victim firing every ~2 s,
so it re-feeds long before the clock runs out. The fade is aimed at
poke-someone-then-wander-off, which is a HUMAN pattern; the bot pattern is
"glued to one target until it dies". Two candidate reasons malady is the one
that shows: it needs only **two** hits where frost and gale need three (so its
pile spends more of its life at 1, one tick from empty), and it is by far the
strongest of the three on this instrument (51-59% against frost/gale's 7-13%),
so the same proportional loss is legible on malady and buried in churn on the
others. ⚠ Either way the number is a **floor**: the game-night complaint ("the
new ice is too strong") is about a play pattern this instrument cannot produce.
Remi's feel, after playing with the 9 s, is the verdict; `STACK_DECAY.seconds`
is the one-line knob.

### 4. Midas: invisible, on both instruments.

**D3-tycoon** (midas 3 + Echo 2 + hourglass, then sword 3 + amulet 2) is at
**−20**, below the +19 line but nowhere near a real move. The elemental study
run with the old penalty agrees: midas 4.0% shipped vs 3.6% old on seed 1, and
2.5% vs 2.8% on seed 7. The seeds **disagree on direction**, so the answer is
"no measurable effect". That makes sense: every roster core that buys midas
takes it to lv3, where `dmgMult` is 1.0 under both specs. The softening only
pays during the climb, and it buys back 20% of one element's damage for a
handful of rounds. Question E stands: midas's value rides on shopping depth,
which the lab floor cannot see.

### 5. The fireball range cap is invisible here, and the reason is not "the cap is too generous".

Nothing in the table has the shape of a sniper nerf. The one true long-line
build, **D9-phantom** (ghost 3 pierce + ember 3), is at +10, dead on the
redistribution line.

Two measurements say the cap cannot show up in a bot table:

- **The mechanism.** Running with the cap lifted and recording how far each
  fireball actually flew before it disappeared (60 lobbies, 61417 balls):
  38.7% travelled 50+ units, but **50.6% ended on a body, and only 0.5% of
  those (143 of 31059) had flown 50 or more**. The long-flight balls were all
  misses sailing into the lava. The cap deletes misses, not damage.
- **A paired ablation.** The same tournament run twice at 2500 games seed 1
  with identical lobbies and game seeds, `SPELLS.fireball.range` 50 against
  Infinity: mean |Δ| **19.9**, max 77, which is the ordinary single-run noise
  floor at that game count. No family lines up: the biggest loser is
  K3-faker-minefield (−77) and the biggest winner is C6-bolt-combo (+45).

Why: `stepBot` casts the fireball whenever it is off cooldown, with **no range
check at all**, and a Hard berserker fights at 5 to 13 units (`standoff` 5,
prowl ring 8.5). Bots were never sniping. ⚠ So the cap's whole effect is on
human play (the "step toward the danger" rule Remi wanted), and this instrument
has nothing to say about it in either direction. Do not read the flat table as
"the cap did nothing".

### 6. C4-boomerang-main slipped 67 and nothing here explains it.

C4 (boomerang 3 + ember 3, then Blood Sword 3 + Health Amulet 3) is still the
best non-Faker row, but it moved −67 against a +19 baseline on both seeds. It
holds none of the six changed things, and the range ablation prices it at −3,
so the cap is not the cause. Most likely it is the arrival of the Faker rows
changing who it meets in the top half. It carried a **⚠bot over-read** flag
before this table and it keeps it: bots catch their own boomerang far more
reliably than a human does.

### 7. The Faker rows are a new instrument fact, not a balance finding.

K1-K4 score **2444 to 2755**, about 1000 Elo above the best Hard-bot strategy
(C4 at 1716), which on this scale is a **99.7% pairwise favourite**. That is the
Faker BRAIN (a tier above Extreme) beating Hard bots, not four strong shopping
lists. They are useful as each other's controls (galeforce and minefield are
level, permafrost ~55 back, hookstorm ~280 back) and useless as a comparison
against any Hard row. ⚠ Their presence is also why every raw Δ in this file
needed re-centring.

## What this instrument cannot see

The standing list, plus what round 22 added:

- **Bots cannot price reactive skill.** No target-leading, no baiting, no CC
  chaining, no reading a bluff. Reactive tools (shield, blink, boomerang
  recall) and malady's contagion read at a floor.
- **Anger saturates it** (question K, three tables running). The 22.5 slow is
  visible as a drop but anger is still 20 points clear of every other element
  at 83-84% against a 25% baseline.
- **The 22.4 stack fade is aimed at a pattern bots do not have** (poke, leave,
  come back). Hard bots re-feed a pile every ~2 s from a ring of 8.5 units.
- **The 22.5 range cap is aimed at a behaviour bots do not have** (shooting
  across the arena). 0.5% of connecting bot fireballs had flown 50+.
- **The 22.5 vampire change cannot be ablated** without reverting `sim.js`.
- Mine and Decoy are not in the roster at all (no bot sets a trap or reads a
  bluff); teams, permanent pillars and arena scaling are absent (every game
  here is a 4-seat free-for-all); Switcheroo combos, vanish timing, Repulse
  baits and the Mirror Wall are human-only.
- Round 22 gave Normal and Hard a `standoff` (13 / 5). Hard's prowl ring is
  unchanged, so this table is comparable to r249 on that axis, but its ladder
  verdict is still awaiting Remi's feel call.

## How to reproduce

```bash
node tools/elo.js --games=8000 --seed=1     # THE table (then --seed=2), ~30 min each
node tools/arena.js --mode=elemental --games=800 --seed=1   # the per-element view (then --seed=7)
node tools/arena.js --mode=elemental --games=800 --seed=1 --fx=anger.markEvery=20,15,10
node tools/arena.js --mode=elemental --games=800 --seed=1 --fx=midas.dmgMult=0.5,0.75,1
```

The stack-fade and fireball-range ablations have no `--fx` path (`STACK_DECAY`
and `SPELLS` are not in arena's override map). Both were run from a four-line
wrapper that imports `shared/constants.js`, mutates the value, then
dynamic-imports the lab so the mutation is in place before the lab's module
body runs. Rebuild it the same way rather than editing constants.js.
