# Strategy ELO — round 21.9 (2026-08-11, r249)

**What this measures.** 37 shopping strategies (`tools/roster.js`) in random
4-of-37 Hard-bot lobbies; a Bradley-Terry fit over every pairwise placement,
scaled so **1500 = the roster average** and **+173 ≈ a 73% pairwise favourite**.
`place` is the mean finish (1-4, lower better). `node tools/elo.js --games=8000
--seed=1` and `--seed=7`, elemental, 0 unfinished games. **Cross-seed drift mean
10.1, max 33** — a gap of ~50 is real, ~10 is noise.

⚠ **Five changes landed between the r236 table and this one**, so no single row
can be attributed to one of them:
1. Blood Sword lifesteal **18/30/38 → 10/20/30%**
2. Slow Spoon **1/1.5/2 → 1/2/3**, and DoT/aura ticks now heal **a tenth** of it
3. Hourglass haste **10/18/26 → 10/20/30**
4. Kill credit: **the 5 s window is gone** — last damage owns the death
5. **DoT ticks stamp the last-hitter slot** (a plague or burn that chases someone
   into the lava now takes the kill)

⚠ And Elo is zero-sum: the fit pins the average at 1500, so every gain is
someone else's loss. `Δ` is versus r236 at the same seed, 35 → 37 strategies.

## Findings

1. **The blade nerf landed exactly where Remi aimed it.** His reason for cutting
   lifesteal was "anger + blade makes you a drain tank". B3-mutation-depth — anger
   maxed, then blade and amulet — takes the **single biggest drop in the table,
   −94**, and its stablemate D2-executioner −58, D1-warlord −50. All three are
   damage builds that converted damage into healing. They are still 1st, 4th and
   6th, so this is a trim, not a demolition.

2. **The sustain shelf moved as a class, and it is the dominant story.**
   A1-items-sustain **+140** (26th → 12th), F1-spoon-volume **+115**,
   D11-spoonbearer **+82**, A2-items-mobility **+80**, D8-juggernaut **+63**,
   D6-sumo **+56**, B5-item-depth **+52**. The Slow Spoon at 1/2/3 plus tick
   healing is a real item now — but note the spoon builds are still **31st and
   34th**: playable, not strong. The item that gained most from the spoon is
   A1, which pairs it with the amulet.

3. **Auras and plague: half of Remi's hypothesis is right.** D12-hatburner
   **+50 → 3rd of 37**, its best placing ever, and the only non-anger build in
   the top four. But D5-plaguebearer (plague without the Hat) moved **+17** —
   inside noise. So it is the *aura*, not the sickness, that gained, and the
   likely mechanism is the Hat's 748 ticks/game each paying a tenth of a spoon
   proc *and* claiming the last-hitter slot. ⚠ This cannot be separated from
   the item changes in one table; a credit-only A/B would need a build with the
   old items.

4. **Every death now lands on a name: 100.0%** (3523 of 3523 deaths in an
   80-game plague-heavy lobby). Before the credit rules changed, a victim
   knocked into the lava at full health burned for ~7 s and outlived the 5 s
   window — the exact scene Remi reported. The share of deaths that are *lava*
   deaths is unchanged (23.8 → 24.2%); what changed is who owns them.

5. **The two spells nobody can pilot stayed put**, as they must: C7-statue-guard
   1499/1514 (dead average, again) and C5-meteor-value last for the third table
   running, unmoved by its lv2 damage buff. The meteor's problem is its cast
   rules, not its numbers.

## What this instrument cannot see

Mine and Decoy (no bot sets a trap or reads a bluff, so neither is in the
roster); malady's contagion and gale's positioning (bots never cluster or aim
shoves); teams, permanent pillars and arena scaling (every game here is a 4-seat
free-for-all); and every human-only spell (Switcheroo combos, vanish timing,
Repulse baits, Mirror Wall).
