## The table

What the numbers are: **Elo** is a Bradley-Terry fit over every pairwise
placement from random 4-strategy lobbies of Hard (berserker) bots in the
elemental ruleset, **8000 games per seed**, run at r219 (round 21 complete:
new spell/item prices, permanent pillars, Coal Brazier, Statue, Decoy, teams).
**1500 = the roster average by construction**, and **+173 Elo ≈ a 73% pairwise
favourite**; `place` is the mean finish 1-4 (lower is better), `cost` is what
the shopping order actually spends, `r20` is the same strategy's seed-1 Elo in
the round-20 table (r207), `Δ` is now minus then. ⚠ **It is a RANKING, not a
strength meter**: the fit pins the average at 1500, so a row can gain 100 Elo
purely because its rivals got worse. Sorted by seed-1 Elo. 0 unfinished games
in both seeds.

## Findings (diffed against the r207 round-20 table)

1. **The top three do not move.** B3-mutation-depth (anger3 first) holds #1 at
   2064/1993 (+27) and D2-executioner (anger built for the chase) holds #3
   (+2). Anger is unmoved by everything round 21 shipped. ⚠ Elo is zero-sum
   here, so this still cannot say whether anger is absolutely too strong —
   question K is Remi's feel report, not this table's.
2. **Half-price spell upgrades (21.1) paid the KIT, not the MAXER.**
   E2-chronomancer +80 → **4th of 30**, the best non-anger row (was 7th);
   A5-spells-only now spends **88g** (was 96g) and rises to 10th; C3-kit-width
   8th. But the three max-one-spell builds are still 24th/27th/28th
   (C1-bolt-rush −27, C6-bolt-combo −60, C5-meteor-value −46): **"don't max
   spells" survived its own price cut.** The C2-vs-C1 gap (lightning lv1 +
   ember vs lightning maxed) is 372 Elo, essentially round-20's ~380.
3. **Items after −1g/level AND the new Coal Brazier: still the bottom.**
   Item-only cores now spend 129g — the *whole* shelf, brazier included — and
   occupy ranks 22, 23, 25, 26 and 29 of 30. Band 1123-1313 vs round-20's
   1099-1341: a tighter floor, not an exit. B6-item-breadth buys the brazier at
   all three levels and *loses* 30 Elo (22nd → 25th). Third consecutive price
   cut with no rank movement: **items are not price-limited, they are
   effect-limited.** Compare A3-elements-only at 1557 on 102g and A5 at 1585
   on 88g.
4. **The biggest movers in the table are the three MALADY builds**:
   D5-plaguebearer **+202** (28th → 21st), A4-mutations-only **+124** (24th →
   20th), B4-mutation-breadth **+109** (19th → 15th). Cause: round 20.3
   (`72d4c8e`), which post-dates the r207 measurement — aura halved
   [10,14,18] → [5,7,9] but the **creator became immune to their own
   instance**. The immunity ruling is worth far more than the aura nerf costs.
   ⚠ And these rows are cluster-blind (below), so the true gain is larger:
   malady is the thing to watch in the next human playtest.
5. **Echo (ex-mosquito) builds stayed demoted at the new prices** — the
   round-20 collapse was structural, not a seed artifact: D3-tycoon 1574 (+3,
   12th), E1-hastemaker 1455 (+17, 18th), D4-leech 1437 (−18, 19th),
   C6-bolt-combo/Chainer 1191 (−60, 27th). "Midas × mosquito is the champion
   economy engine" stays **VOID**; nothing in round-21 pricing rescued the pair.
6. **Element strategies gave ground purely by displacement**: A3-elements-only
   −116 (10th → 13th), B2-element-breadth −84, B1-element-depth −63. Round 21
   touched no element price or effect — the mutation and kit builds rising past
   them is exactly the zero-sum artifact the ⚠ caveat warns about. Do not read
   these as element nerfs.
7. **Round 21's headline mechanisms are invisible here, by construction.**
   Permanent pillars: bots never buy pillars, so no lab has yet seen the
   permanence fortress. Statue 🗿 and Decoy 👥: `tier: 'power'`, filtered from
   bots, absent from the roster. Teams: Elo lobbies are free-for-all. Arena
   scaling: inert at 4 seats (`startRadius` only grows above 5). **This table
   measures round 21's PRICES; everything round 21 shipped that a human pilots
   is still unmeasured.**
8. **Stability.** 0 unfinished games in both seeds. Mean cross-seed |Δ| is
   **13.5 Elo**, max **71** — and the max is B3, the top row, because the
   extremes of a Bradley-Terry fit are the least constrained (round 20 reported
   26). Treat < ~70 Elo at the very top and < ~25 elsewhere as noise. Seed 2's
   order is identical bar adjacent swaps (A5/D10, B2/D10, D3/A3, A4/D5).

## Blind spots (state these next to any quote from the table)

- **C4-boomerang-main's #2 is a bot artifact.** Nothing in the lab dodges or
  catches a boomerang; this row is over-read in every tournament so far.
- **Malady rows are cluster-blind** (D5, A4, B4): bots never bunch up, so
  contagion measures at a floor. Gale's gust positioning is unexpressible the
  same way (B2, A3). **D6-sumo is position-blind** — knockback needs a pilot
  who aims at lava.
- **Statue and Decoy would measure at the ~3% do-nothing floor if added to the
  roster — BY DESIGN.** That is the instrument being honest about a reactive
  tool, not the spell being weak. Human playtest is the only instrument.
