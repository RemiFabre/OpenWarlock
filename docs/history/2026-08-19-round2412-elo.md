## Reading (vs the 24.10 standard table; +/-40 = noise)

- **D1-warlord 1598 -> 1604**: the anchor held; deltas below are real.
- **M4-echo-first 1572 -> 1579**: the consistency probe did not move; the
  echo cadence is byte-identical to 24.10, as intended.
- **Anger rows UP on Hard**: M1-anger-first 1529 -> 1614, D2-executioner
  1117 -> 1440, D14-hyperscaler 1236 -> 1338. Hard bots now release 50-100%
  of the bank (mean ~75%) where the 24.9 bar paid a spammer ~50%, and the
  +size makes their committed balls land more. K5-faker-vendetta 2621 ->
  2537 (a perfect 1.5 s hold delays the combo layer's next step; small,
  possibly noise).
- **The 24.11 trims read as intended, mild**: M5-midas-first 1504 -> 1442
  (coin chance -5pt at lv3 + the 10 s melt), D4-leech 1654 -> 1591 and
  M6-vampire-first (lowHpMax 3 -> 2.5).
- **What the instrument CANNOT see** (flag, not a number to fix): bots
  never deliberately release EARLY to dodge-cancel or to bait, never hold
  through a fight to threaten, and the re-aim lead is first-order (no
  human-style curve reads). The charge's skill ceiling (release timing as a
  mind game) is priced at zero on both tables. Run A vs Run B brackets the
  tracking skill alone at roughly +500-800 elo on anger rows.
